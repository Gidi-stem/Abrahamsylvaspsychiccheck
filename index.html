The story behind the gift

He listens past the words to the symbol beneath

Abraham is based in Africa, where dreams are treated as messages rather than noise. They arrive in symbols, and those symbols have been read and passed down for generations. That heritage shaped his gift.

Over fifteen years he has learned to hear what sits under what a person says: a dream that keeps returning, a feeling that arrives before a decision, a blockage nobody can explain. People come to him in the middle of change, unsure which way to turn.

He has now completed more than 80 psychic appointments and interpreted 20 dreams. Each one begins the same way. You speak, he listens, and the symbols start to answer. Then he turns what they reveal into steps you can take.

About

Ancient symbolic insight, practical life strategy

Abraham Sylvas is a seasoned Spiritual Counselor, Intuitive Reader and Oneiromancer based in Africa. Grounded in rich cultural heritage and deep intuitive wisdom, he specializes in dream interpretation, soul alignment and spiritual guidance for individuals navigating life transitions, internal blockages and personal awakenings.
